what even is a fanlisting?


A fanlisting is typically an online listing made by a fan of something or someone for other fans of that subject to join. On these fanlistings, you will usually see the members' email, websites (if they own one), the country they're from, and their name. Basically it's a group of people listed online who all share the same interest.
fanlistings are very fun because you can easily connect with fans of the same interest and show off to others that you're apart of something really cool!
